## What You Will Learn
- Configure Protocol Independent Multicast (PIM) sparse-mode and dense-mode operations on Junos devices
- Implement Source-Specific Multicast (SSM) for controlled receiver group management and RPF verification

- Troubleshoot multicast forwarding states, join/prune messages, and reverse path forwarding issues
- Integrate multicast routing with BGP for inter-domain anycast RP and scoped address allocation
