## What You Will Learn
- Configure and optimize OSPF and IS-IS for large-scale service provider topologies, including multi-area designs and fast convergence
- Implement BGP route reflection, confederations, and policy-based routing for inter-domain scalability
- Deploy MPLS protocols such as LDP, RSVP-TE, and traffic engineering for efficient label-switched paths
- Build and troubleshoot Layer 2 VPNs (L2VPN) and Layer 3 VPNs (L3VPN) with VRFs and route distinguishers
- Apply Class of Service (CoS) mechanisms for traffic classification, queuing, and shaping in QoS-aware networks
