## What You Will Learn
- Conduct comprehensive risk assessments using frameworks like ISO 27005 to identify vulnerabilities in physical and digital data center environments.
- Design and implement layered security controls, including access management, encryption, and intrusion detection systems.
- Evaluate environmental threats such as power failures, cooling issues, and natural disasters with mitigation planning.
- Develop incident response and business continuity plans tailored to data center operations.
- Ensure compliance with standards like PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and SOC 2 through auditing and reporting techniques.
