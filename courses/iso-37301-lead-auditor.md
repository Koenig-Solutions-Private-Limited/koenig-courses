## What You Will Learn
- Interpret ISO 37301 clauses to assess compliance management system effectiveness, including leadership commitment and policy integration
- Apply ISO 19011 principles for planning risk-based audits, defining objectives, and selecting audit teams
- Conduct on-site and remote audits, gathering evidence through interviews, document reviews, and observation techniques
- Analyze non-conformities, root causes, and opportunities for improvement during audit execution
- Compile audit reports, recommend corrective actions, and manage follow-up processes for certification maintenance
