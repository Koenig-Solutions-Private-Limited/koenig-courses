Data centres underpin global digital infrastructure, with demand exploding due to AI and edge computing. Holding Data Centre Foundation certification opens doors to roles like data centre technicians, facilities engineers, and operations managers. It boosts career progression by demonstrating competency in critical uptime and efficiency, where certified pros command higher salaries and lead resilient deployments.

## What You Will Learn
- Core data centre components, including server racks, cabling infrastructure, and modular designs
- Power systems fundamentals, such as UPS, PDUs, and redundancy tiers (N+1, 2N)
- Cooling methodologies, from CRAC units to liquid cooling and hot/cold aisle containment
- Physical security protocols, access controls, CCTV integration, and environmental monitoring
- Industry standards like TIA-942 for layout, certification, and tier classifications

## Who Should Attend
Entry-level IT staff, facilities technicians, network operators, and aspiring data centre managers with minimal prior exposure. Suited for sysadmins transitioning to infrastructure roles or support engineers seeking foundational data centre operations knowledge.
