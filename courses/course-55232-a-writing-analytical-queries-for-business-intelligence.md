## What You Will Learn
- Master the GROUP BY extensions including GROUPING SETS, ROLLUP, and CUBE for multidimensional aggregations in BI reports.
- Implement window functions like ROW_NUMBER, RANK, DENSE_RANK, LAG, and LEAD using the OVER clause for ranking and sequential analysis.
- Apply the OFFSET-FETCH clause with ORDER BY for efficient pagination in large-scale analytical datasets.
- Utilize TOP with TIES and PERCENT to handle variable result sets in dynamic BI queries.
- Optimize analytical queries with CHECKSUM aggregation and advanced indexing strategies for performance in SQL Server environments.
