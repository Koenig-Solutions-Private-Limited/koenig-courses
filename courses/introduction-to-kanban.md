## What You Will Learn
- Core Kanban principles: Understand just-in-time production, flow optimization, and evolutionary change for sustainable processes.
- Building Kanban boards: Design digital and physical boards with columns for To Do, In Progress, and Done stages.
- WIP limits and policies: Implement work-in-progress constraints to prevent overload and highlight bottlenecks.
- Metrics and visualization: Track lead time, cycle time, and throughput using cumulative flow diagrams.
- Continuous improvement: Apply retrospectives, experiments, and feedback loops to refine workflows iteratively.
