Designed for experienced IT service professionals, it addresses the need for seamless transitions amid rising demands for compliant, agile IT services in hybrid environments. ISO/IEC 20000 Transition certification validates expertise in upholding international ITSM standards, critical for organizational resilience.

Organizations worldwide prioritize ISO/IEC 20000 Transition to align with updated standards, driving demand for certified experts in IT service management. ## What You Will Learn
- Key structural shifts from ISO/IEC 20000-1:2011 to the 2018 version, including High-Level Structure (HLS) adoption
- Updating leadership and planning clauses for enhanced context understanding and risk-based thinking
- Revising support processes like resource allocation, competence, and awareness under new mandates
- Adapting operation and control requirements for service delivery and incident management
- Implementing performance evaluation, including monitoring, audits, and management reviews for certification readiness
