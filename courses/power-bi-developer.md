## What You Will Learn

- Master DAX for advanced measures, time intelligence functions, and dynamic aggregations

- Develop interactive reports and dashboards with drill-throughs, bookmarks, and AI visuals
- Manage workspaces, deploy datasets to Power BI Service, and configure row-level security
