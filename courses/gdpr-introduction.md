## What You Will Learn
- Core GDPR principles: lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, and confidentiality
- Data subject rights, including access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction, portability, and objection
- Distinctions between data controllers and processors, with accountability obligations like record-keeping and contracts
- Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) and managing lawful bases for processing personal data
- Breach notification protocols, response timelines, and strategies for international data transfers under GDPR

## Who Should Attend
IT administrators, compliance coordinators, HR managers, and business analysts with little to no prior privacy experience. Suited for entry-level professionals or those transitioning into data governance roles in EU-impacted organizations.
