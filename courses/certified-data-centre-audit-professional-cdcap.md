## What You Will Learn
- Master data centre audit methodologies, including site surveys and documentation protocols aligned with ISO 27001 and Uptime Institute tiers.
- Conduct risk assessments for power, cooling, and cabling systems to pinpoint single points of failure.
- Evaluate physical security measures, such as access controls, CCTV integration, and perimeter defences.
- Analyse environmental controls like HVAC efficiency, fire suppression, and humidity management against BICSI standards.
- Develop audit reports with actionable recommendations, benchmarking against TIA-942 rated facilities.
