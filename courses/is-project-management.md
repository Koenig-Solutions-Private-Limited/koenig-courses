## What You Will Learn
- Develop project charters and define objectives tailored to information systems environments
- Construct work breakdown structures (WBS) and schedule IS project activities using tools like MS Project
- Implement risk registers and perform qualitative/quantitative analysis for IT project uncertainties
- Apply earned value management (EVM) techniques to monitor IS project performance and variances
- Facilitate change control processes and conduct post-project reviews for continuous improvement

## Who Should Attend
IT coordinators, systems analysts, junior project managers, and business analysts with 1-3 years in tech roles. Ideal for those seeking to lead IS initiatives or advance from technical contributor positions.
