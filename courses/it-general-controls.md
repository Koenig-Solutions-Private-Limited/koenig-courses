## What You Will Learn
- Implement logical access controls, including user provisioning, segregation of duties, and privileged access management.
- Design and audit change management processes to minimize disruptions and ensure configuration integrity.
- Establish IT operations controls for backup, recovery, and incident response to maintain system availability.
- Evaluate physical and environmental security measures, such as data center access and climate controls.
- Apply system development lifecycle controls to govern software acquisition, development, and maintenance.

## Who Should Attend
