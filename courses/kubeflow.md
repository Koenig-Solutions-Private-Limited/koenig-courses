## What You Will Learn
- Deploy Kubeflow components on Kubernetes clusters, including metadata stores and multi-tenancy setups

- Manage Jupyter notebooks in Kubeflow for collaborative experimentation and versioning
- Serve trained models at scale via KServe, supporting inference graphs and autoscaling
