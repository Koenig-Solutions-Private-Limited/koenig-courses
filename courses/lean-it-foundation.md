## What You Will Learn
- Identify and categorize the eight types of waste specific to IT processes, such as overproduction and waiting in service desks.
- Map value streams to visualize IT workflows, pinpointing bottlenecks in incident management and change processes.
- Apply Kaizen techniques for rapid, incremental improvements in IT service delivery and infrastructure support.
- Define Lean metrics like cycle time and throughput to measure and sustain IT process performance.
- Integrate Lean principles with ITIL frameworks for enhanced service management and agile alignment.
