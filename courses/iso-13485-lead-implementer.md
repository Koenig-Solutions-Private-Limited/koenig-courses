## What You Will Learn
- Interpret ISO 13485 clauses, focusing on regulatory requirements for medical device design, production, and servicing.
- Develop risk-based QMS implementation plans, incorporating hazard analysis and traceability controls.
- Establish leadership commitment, resource allocation, and competence frameworks for effective QMS support.
- Implement operational controls, including validation, supplier management, and post-market surveillance.
- Conduct internal audits, management reviews, and continual improvement using PDCA cycles for certification readiness.
