## What You Will Learn
- Core principles of Lean methodology, focusing on value stream identification and flow optimization
- Six Sigma basics, including variation reduction and defect measurement using simple statistical concepts
- The eight wastes (TIMWOODS) and techniques for their detection in IT processes
- Introduction to DMAIC framework: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control phases
- Basic tools like SIPOC diagrams for high-level process mapping and stakeholder analysis
