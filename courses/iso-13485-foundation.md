# ISO 13485 Foundation — Training & Certification Guide

It equips participants with knowledge of regulatory requirements, risk management, and process controls essential for compliance. Aimed at quality professionals, engineers, and regulatory staff new to the field, ISO 13485 Foundation training addresses the growing need in healthcare IT for safe, traceable device development amid stricter global regulations and supply chain scrutiny.

## Why This Certification Matters
Demand for ISO 13485 Foundation expertise surges in medical device manufacturing, driven by FDA and EU MDR mandates. Key roles like quality engineers, compliance analysts, and validation specialists benefit, as this credential validates skills in QMS implementation. It elevates career trajectories by opening doors to leadership in audits and continual improvement, amid a sector expanding with innovative diagnostics and wearables.

## What You Will Learn
- Structure and mandatory clauses of the ISO 13485:2016 standard for medical device QMS
- Application of risk-based thinking to design, development, and production processes
- Requirements for documentation control, records management, and traceability
- Controls for supplier evaluation, purchasing, and outsourced processes
- Basics of internal audits, non-conformity handling, and management reviews

## Who Should Attend
Quality assurance technicians, medical device engineers, regulatory affairs associates, and process coordinators with entry-level experience or none in QMS. Ideal for professionals entering medical device compliance or supporting IT-integrated manufacturing environments.
