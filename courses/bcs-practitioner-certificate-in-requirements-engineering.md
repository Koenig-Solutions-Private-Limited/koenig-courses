## What You Will Learn
- Apply elicitation techniques such as workshops, interviews, and prototyping to gather comprehensive stakeholder needs.
- Use UML and entity-relationship models to analyse and document requirements with precision.
- Prioritise requirements using MoSCoW and analytical hierarchy process for optimal resource allocation.
- Perform requirements validation through reviews, walkthroughs, and traceability matrices.
- Implement requirements management practices, including versioning, change control, and tool integration like Jira or DOORS.
