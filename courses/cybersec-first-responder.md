With cyber threats surging—over 2,200 daily attacks per organization—the CyberSec First Responder certification validates expertise in rapid incident handling. ## What You Will Learn
- Identify and classify cybersecurity threats using indicators of compromise (IOCs) and attack vectors like phishing and DDoS.
- Conduct forensic investigations on endpoints, memory dumps, and network traffic with tools such as Volatility and Wireshark.
- Perform vulnerability assessments and prioritize remediation using CVSS scoring and patch management workflows.
- Analyze malware behaviors through static and dynamic techniques, including sandboxing and reverse engineering basics.
- Develop and execute incident response playbooks, including containment, eradication, and post-incident reporting per NIST SP 800-61.

## Who Should Attend
This course suits IT support specialists, network administrators, and junior security analysts with 1-2 years of experience in systems or networking. Security operations center (SOC) team members and help desk professionals seeking to pivot into incident response will benefit most from its hands-on focus.
