## What You Will Learn
- Diagnose ACI fabric discovery issues, including leaf/spine initialization and underlay connectivity using atomic counters
- Troubleshoot endpoint group (EPG) registration failures and resolve VLAN/BD mapping discrepancies
- Analyze contract enforcement problems, microsegmentation faults, and filter match violations via fault trees
- Investigate APIC cluster health, controller replication errors, and snapshot-based recovery procedures
- Leverage NX-OS style CLI, Visore, and MO query tools for advanced diagnostics and log analysis
