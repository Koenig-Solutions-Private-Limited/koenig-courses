## What You Will Learn
- ACI fabric design principles, including spine-leaf topology, underlay protocols like IS-IS, and VXLAN overlay for scalable multi-pod/pod fabrics
- Tenant provisioning, endpoint groups (EPGs), and contracts for policy-based microsegmentation and application-centric security
- APIC controller configuration for automation, including REST APIs, Ansible integration, and visore for troubleshooting
- Integration with L4-L7 services, external networks via border leaves, and vCenter for virtualized environments
- Monitoring tools like atomic counters, fault management, and health scores for operational ACI deployments

## Who Should Attend
