## What You Will Learn
- Fundamental principles of Layer 2 VPNs, including circuit cross-connect (CCC), translational cross-connect (TCC), and Layer 2 circuits in Junos OS
- BGP-signaled L2VPN configurations for point-to-point and multipoint Ethernet services
- VPLS implementation, including Martini and Kompella control planes with MAC learning and flooding mechanisms
- Ethernet VPN (EVPN) deployment for next-generation multipoint Layer 2 fabrics over MPLS or VXLAN
- Advanced troubleshooting techniques using Junos tools like show commands, traceoptions, and commit scripts for L2VPN failures
