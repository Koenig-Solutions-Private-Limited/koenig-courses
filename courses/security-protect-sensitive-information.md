## What You Will Learn
- Classify information assets using standards like NIST or ISO 27001 to prioritize protection efforts
- Implement cryptographic controls such as AES encryption and key management for data at rest and in transit
- Design access control models including RBAC and ABAC to enforce least privilege principles
- Develop data loss prevention (DLP) strategies to monitor and block unauthorized exfiltration
- Establish secure disposal methods like cryptographic erasure and physical destruction for end-of-life data
