# ICS/SCADA Cybersecurity — Training & Certification Guide

Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) form the backbone of critical infrastructure, yet they face escalating cyber threats from nation-states and ransomware. This [ICS/SCADA Cybersecurity](https://www.koenig-solutions.com/ics-scada-cybersecurity-training?utm_source=github&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=github-publisher) course equips professionals with defenses against these risks, covering threat landscapes, secure configurations, and resilience strategies. Ideal for OT engineers and security specialists, it addresses why ICS/SCADA Cybersecurity is vital in protecting energy, water, and manufacturing sectors amid rising attacks.

## Why This Certification Matters
Demand for ICS/SCADA Cybersecurity expertise surges in critical infrastructure, with roles like OT security engineers and control system analysts commanding premium salaries. Breaches like Colonial Pipeline highlight vulnerabilities, making certified pros essential for compliance with NIST and IEC standards, boosting career mobility in utilities and manufacturing.

## What You Will Learn
- Analyze ICS architectures, including Purdue Model levels and protocols like Modbus, DNP3, and OPC UA
- Identify OT-specific vulnerabilities, such as legacy system weaknesses and air-gapped network myths
- Design secure ICS networks using segmentation, firewalls, and DMZ configurations
- Perform risk assessments with tools like threat modeling and vulnerability scanning tailored to SCADA
- Develop incident response plans for ICS disruptions, including forensics and recovery procedures

## Who Should Attend
Control system engineers, OT cybersecurity analysts, industrial network administrators, and system integrators with foundational networking or automation experience. Suited for those transitioning from IT to operational technology security roles.
