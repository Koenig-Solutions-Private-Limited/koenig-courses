## What You Will Learn
- Core principles of data center site selection, including environmental factors and risk assessment
- Power infrastructure design, covering UPS systems, generators, and redundancy tiers (N+1, 2N)
- Thermal management techniques, such as CRAC units, hot/cold aisle containment, and liquid cooling basics
- Structured cabling standards (e.g., TIA-942) and fiber optic deployment for high-density racks
- Physical security layers, from access controls and CCTV to fire suppression and disaster recovery planning

## Who Should Attend
