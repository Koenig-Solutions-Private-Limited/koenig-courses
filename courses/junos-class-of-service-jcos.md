# Junos Class of Service (JCOS) — Training & Certification Guide

The [Junos Class of Service (JCOS)](https://www.koenig-solutions.com/junos-class-of-service-jcos-training?utm_source=github&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=github-publisher) course delivers in-depth training on implementing Quality of Service (QoS) features within Juniper Networks devices using Junos OS. ## What You Will Learn
- Configure behavior aggregate (BA) and multifield (MF) classifiers to identify and mark traffic based on headers and protocols
- Implement rewrite rules and forwarding classes to direct packets into appropriate queues for differential treatment
- Deploy schedulers with transmission rate limits, buffer sizes, and priority levels for precise bandwidth allocation
- Set up two- and three-color policers to enforce rate limiting and discard or remark excess traffic
- Design and verify hierarchical Class of Service (CoS) structures for complex, multi-level queuing on aggregated interfaces

## Who Should Attend
Network engineers, Juniper administrators, and QoS specialists with foundational Junos OS experience (e.g., JNCIA-Junos certified) seeking to master traffic engineering. Ideal for those in enterprise or service provider roles managing high-traffic backbones.
