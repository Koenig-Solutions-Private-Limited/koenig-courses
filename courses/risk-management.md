## What You Will Learn
- Risk identification methods, including threat modeling and vulnerability scanning in IT infrastructures
- Qualitative and quantitative risk analysis techniques, such as probability-impact matrices and Monte Carlo simulations
- Risk response strategies, covering avoidance, mitigation, transfer, and acceptance with real-world IT case studies
- Monitoring and control processes using key risk indicators and dashboards for ongoing oversight
- Integration of risk management into enterprise frameworks like ISO 31000 and COBIT for IT governance

## Who Should Attend
