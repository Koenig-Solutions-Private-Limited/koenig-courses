## Why This Certification Matters
Data centres underpin global digital infrastructure, with demand for skilled technicians rising due to hyperscale expansions and sustainability mandates. The Certified Data Centre Technician Professional (CDCTP) opens doors to roles like data centre operator, facilities engineer, and critical infrastructure specialist, boosting employability and salary potential in a sector projected to grow exponentially.

## What You Will Learn
- Structured cabling systems, including fibre optics, copper standards, and patch panel configurations for optimal signal integrity.
- Power infrastructure management, encompassing UPS sizing, PDU deployment, and redundancy strategies like N+1 configurations.
- Environmental controls, focusing on CRAC units, hot/cold aisle containment, and precision air conditioning for thermal efficiency.
- Physical security measures, such as biometric access, CCTV integration, and perimeter defence aligned with Tier standards.
- Rack and stack procedures, including equipment installation, cable management, and compliance with Uptime Institute guidelines.

## Who Should Attend
Data centre technicians, IT support engineers, facilities managers, and network cabling specialists with 1–3 years of hands-on experience in server rooms or enterprise IT environments will benefit most from this professional-level training.
