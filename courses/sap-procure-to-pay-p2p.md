## What You Will Learn
- Configure and manage master data for materials, vendors, and purchasing organizations in SAP MM.
- Create, approve, and convert procurement requisitions into requests for quotation (RFQs) and purchase orders.
- Execute goods receipt processes, including quality inspections and inventory postings with GR/IR clearing.
- Perform three-way matching for invoice verification, handling variances and credit memos.
- Run payment proposals, execute payment runs, and integrate P2P with SAP FI for accurate financial postings.
