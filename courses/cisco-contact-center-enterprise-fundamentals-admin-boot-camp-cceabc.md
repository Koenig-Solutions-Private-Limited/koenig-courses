## What You Will Learn
- Configure Cisco Unified Contact Center Enterprise (UCCE) with Intelligent Contact Management (ICM) for precise call routing and scripting
- Deploy and administer Customer Voice Portal (CVP) for self-service IVR applications and media resource control
- Implement VXML gateways and SIP proxy servers to enable voice XML-based treatments and trunking
- Manage peripheral gateways (PGs) for integrating ACDs, IVRs, and network interfaces in hybrid deployments
- Perform diagnostic troubleshooting and real-time monitoring using diagnostic tools and reports in UCCE environments

## Who Should Attend
