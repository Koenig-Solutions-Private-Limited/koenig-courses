## What You Will Learn
- Apply the COBIT 5 Process Assessment Model (PAM) to evaluate IT processes against capability levels.
- Conduct capability assessments using ISO/IEC 15504 principles for consistent, repeatable results.
- Perform process dimension and attribute ratings to identify strengths and gaps.
- Use enabler performance management for holistic governance assessments.
- Generate and interpret assessment reports to drive remediation and continuous improvement.
